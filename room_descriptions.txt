Quarry
Following rumors of a huge discovery at an excavation site, you 
finally arrive at the rumored quarry. In the distance, you can 
see tunnels dug into the side of the mountain with a steady 
stream of workers hauling chunks of stone. You're more concerned 
with what's right in front of you: a suspiciously flat section 
of rock with an old leather satchel resting on the ground. 

You will eventually open the exit down into the temple. 
#
Antechamber
Climbing down the ladder, you emerge into a room made from 
beautifully carved polished stone. The room clearly serves 
as an antechamber for whatever this structure is. Other than 
the ladder you used, the central pillar is covered in a 
mosaic depicting the elements: fire on the south side, air 
on the west side, water to the north, and earth to the east. 

A doorway leads out of the room on each of the four cardinal 
directions.
#
Outer Cave
This cave has a window carved into the rock, cleverly 
crafted to let light in without being visible from the outside. 
A beam of sunlight streams in, forming a bright spot on the floor. 
You notice a beautiful painting depicting the moon, sun, and stars 
on one of the walls. 

The room has one doorway, leading east back to the antechamber.
#
Torch Room
This room is warmer than the rest of the temple. The floor 
is covered in a massive charcoal etching of a flame, and four  
unlit torches hang in sconces, one on each of the square room's 
four walls. 

The room has one doorway on the north wall, leading back to 
the antechamber.
#
Reflecting Pool
The air in this room is cool, and the stones on the floor are 
slightly damp. In the center of the room, a large circular pool 
of water sits perfectly still. You can see the room vividly reflected 
on the surface of the water, though you can't make out any details 
without a closer look. 

The door on the south wall leads back to the antechamber, 
and you will eventually open the way down to the submerged vault. 
#
Submerged Vault
The vault is surprisingly dry for having been under a pool. 
Resting on a block of clay in front of you is a beautiful white 
rose, somehow preserved since whenever this temple was abandoned. 

You can climb the ladder back up to the pool room.
#
Shrine
A massive statue of a regal figure you don't recognize dominates this room. 
The statue holds a rose in its outstretched hand. At the statue's feet, an 
altar is adorned with what you can only assume are holy symbols. As you enter 
the room, what initially appears to be a smaller statue steps away from the 
wall and positions itself in front of the rear exit. 

You can exit west back to the antechamber, or you can talk 
to the golem and eventually proceed east to the treasure room.
#
Treasure Room
The room at the center of the temple is beautiful, opulent without being 
garish. A chest sits in front of you with something engraved on a plaque 
(there will eventually be a super cool riddle here). Inside the chest, the 
temple's treasure waits for you. 

If for some reason you want to leave without the treasure, you can 
exit west back to the shrine.
